HOW IT WORKS

  1. 01

    COPY 3 PAGES

    Attendance, Time Table and Academic Calendar from VTOP. Select, copy, paste. The setup page shows exactly where each copy starts and ends.

  2. 02

    CROSS OFF DAYS

    Tap a date to skip the whole day, or open it to skip one class. Labs count as 2 (L7+L8), like VTOP counts them.

  3. 03

    STAY ≥ 75%

    Every course shows its projected final %, how many more you can skip, and your longest break. Save Plan A and Plan B to compare.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

Everything runs in your browser. Your scrapes and plans are saved only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

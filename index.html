    HOW IT WORKS

    1. 01

      COPY 3 PAGES

      Attendance, Time Table and Academic Calendar from VTOP. Select, copy, paste. The setup page shows exactly where each copy starts and ends.

    2. 02

      CROSS OFF DAYS

      Tap a date to skip the whole day, or open it to skip one class. Labs count as 2 (L7+L8), like VTOP counts them.

    3. 03

      STAY ≥ 75%

      Every course shows its projected final %, how many more you can skip, and your longest break. Save Plan A and Plan B to compare.

    PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

    Everything runs in your browser and is saved in this browser's local storage. Log in with Google only if you want your plan on your phone and laptop too: it is then stored in your own row in the database, which only your account can read.

    Not affiliated with VIT or VTOP. Always check the official numbers on VTOP. Built for students, by a student.
